About Scholar's Haven

Who we are

Scholar's Haven is a web based book store made for students, teachers and lifelong learners. We keep academic books, programming manuals and general reading in one simple catalog.

  • Books for BCA, BBA, B.Com and B.Sc (IT) courses
  • Simple cart and checkout
  • Student inquiry for any book that is not listed
Our mission

To make academic books and curriculum resources easy to find and easy to order.
